Coworking, Office, Meeting
and Event Space in Portland

↓

Kiln is a community-driven flexible office space for rent in Portland that merges the vibrant pulse of the city with the distinctive feel of the Buckman and Hawthorne neighborhoods.

Kiln Portland merges work and lifestyle, enhances performance, and inspires creativity in its members. The 36,000 sq ft facility is located in Southeast Portland, off the iconic Hawthorne Blvd.

Membership includes access to private work studios, conference rooms, a podcast and recording studio, theater space, a wellness studio, food and beverage offerings, and a diverse calendar of networking, wellness, and lifestyle events and experiences.

Events, Clubs, & Experiences

Kiln Portland offers an unprecedented calendar of hand-curated events, clubs, and experiences to empower you to feel and perform at your very best. Step into our plant-filled spaces, take a yoga class at lunch, meet like-minded professionals, and wrap your day networking at Après Work or Wine Tasting experiences — all available in our Portland coworking space.

Handcrafted Spaces

At Kiln, we believe that workspaces should be both functional and beautiful, that is why we handcraft and design our spaces with thoughtfulness and purpose. Whether you choose to work at one of our shared desks in our club areas, a dedicated desk with lockable storage space for our resident members, or in a private lockable office or large studio, you’ll be surrounded by handcrafted details and finishes that elevate your work environment and inspire productivity.

As a navigator, Lulu works one-on-one with founders to understand where they are, surface what they need most right now, and connect them to the right people, resources, and opportunities to move forward. Finding relevant communities, networks, and peer support
Exploring customer, traction, or growth challenges
Navigating pivots, transitions, or moments of uncertainty
Mapping realistic next steps when things feel unclear

Who Should Attend:

Portland Community FAQs

What is the cost to rent office space in Portland? ↓

In general, the average cost to rent office space in Portland is about $24.10 per square foot. However, it’s important to remember that this average will be highly dependent on various factors, including the office’s precise location and lease duration, as well as its size, layout, and the services available to it. When it comes to Kiln, rental costs are based on the type of space you’re looking to rent and how long you rent it. Our membership has flexible options for private offices and coworking spaces to ensure you’ll get exactly what you need from your particular workspace. Contact us to learn more about pricing and availability.

Does Kiln have private office space for rent in Portland? ↓

Yes! Kiln provides private offices for rent in Portland, built to handle anywhere from 1 to 15 people. Our private offices are enclosed and self-contained, with lockable doors, giving your team a professional workplace with added security.

These private offices are move-in ready and come fully furnished, as well as being equipped with reliable high-speed internet, scanning and printing services, and a whole range of valuable amenities.

Does Kiln offer enterprise office space solutions in Portland? ↓

Yes, Kiln offers office solutions that are customized for your organization’s specific needs. Depending on the location, we have large office spaces allowing enterprise teams of up to 100 people to collaborate, promote productivity, and jumpstart growth. Kiln also provides access to many useful amenities, from high-speed internet, printing and scanning, conference rooms, and much more. Set up a tour with Kiln to explore our enterprise office space for rent in Portland.
